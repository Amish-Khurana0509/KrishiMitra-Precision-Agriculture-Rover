## **KrishiMitra: Har Kisan Ka Sacha Sathi**

**\~An AI-Powered Robot to Detect and Treat Crop Diseases Smartly**

### **The Brief**

Tomato farming in India faces a hidden crisis \- crop diseases silently wipe out produce, and most farmers don’t know they’ve struck until it’s too late. Farmers often respond by spraying pesticides across the entire field, hoping to save their crops. But this practice is costly, harmful, and ineffective. It damages healthy plants, pollutes soil and water, affects human health, and wastes expensive chemicals.

India produces over 20.7 million tonnes of tomatoes annually. Yet, around **35% of this is lost every year** to pests and diseases, causing major income loss to small farmers who make up over 80% of the growers.

That’s where **KrishiMitra** steps in—a Made-in-India, AI-powered farming robot that detects diseases early using a camera and treats only the affected plants. It uses artificial intelligence and robotics to bring a precision-based, eco-friendly solution to rural farmlands.

### **Problem Statement: Why Farmers Need Smart Solutions Now**

* In India, **82% of tomato farmers are smallholders** (owning less than 2 hectares), lacking access to costly tools and expert consultants.  
* **35% of tomato yield** is lost annually to diseases like leaf spots, wilting, and fruit borers.  
* **Widespread chemical spraying** not only wastes resources but damages the environment and affects farmers' health.  
* Farmers currently have **no affordable way** to detect crop diseases in the early stages, leading to low harvests and high input costs.

### **What KrishiMitra Aims to Do**

**KrishiMitra** is an AI-powered robot designed to detect plant diseases in tomato crops and spray pesticide only where it’s needed. By identifying affected areas and targeting only infected plants, it:

* Reduces pesticide use by up to **50%**  
* Prevents disease spread through **early detection**  
* **Increases income** by ensuring more healthy produce  
* Is designed to be **low-cost**, modular, and locally repairable

The project uses Computer Vision Domain of AI to identify diseases and a smart microcontroller system to selectively spray affected plants.

**Why Smart Spraying Is the Future of Agriculture**

* **Targets only sick plants** instead of spraying the entire field.  
* **Minimizes chemical waste**, protecting soil and water bodies.  
* **Reduces input costs**, making farming more profitable.  
* **Protects farmers’ health** by reducing chemical exposure.  
* Ensures **early-stage treatment**, saving crops from widespread infection.

### **Technologies and Tools Used**

* **Raspberry Pi** – Runs the AI model for disease detection  
* **Arduino Uno** – Controls motors and the spraying system  
* **Camera Module** – Captures live images of plant leaves  
* **AI Image Classifier** – Identifies disease symptoms like spots, blights, and discoloration  
* **Mini Pump / Solenoid Valve** – Delivers precise pesticide doses  
* **Chassis, Wheels, and Motors** – Makes the robot mobile  
* **Battery or Solar Panel** – Ensures off-grid operation in rural areas

### **How the AI Model Works – Step by Step**

1. **Image Collection**  
   * The camera scans tomato plant leaves for early signs of disease.  
2. **AI Image Analysis**  
   * A custom-trained AI model running on Raspberry Pi detects patterns of leaf damage and classifies them.  
3. **Disease Detection**  
   * When disease is detected, it sends a signal to the Arduino controller.  
4. **Smart Spraying**  
   * The Arduino activates the pump/valve to spray pesticide directly onto the infected leaf.  
5. **Continuous Operation**  
   * The robot keeps moving and scanning until the field is covered.
